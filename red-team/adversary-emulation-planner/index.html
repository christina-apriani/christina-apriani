What ATLAS Produces

ATLAS turns a threat hypothesis into a defensible, scoped emulation plan. Pick a documented adversary profile or build from zero, assign TTPs across the 15 ATT&CK v20 tactics, and attach a rule-of-engagement and safety control to every behavior.

  • ✓
    De-confliction & safety gates
    no behavior enters execution without a control
  • ✓
    Portable artifact
    export to JSON or print for approval sign-off
Operating principle. ATLAS is a planning and coordination surface. It documents which adversary behaviors an authorized engagement will emulate and how defenders should see them. It holds no exploit code, payloads, or execution tooling. Run only against systems you are explicitly authorized to test, under signed rules of engagement.
