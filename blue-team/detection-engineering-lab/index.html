How SENTINEL works

Every rule is authored in Sigma, the vendor-agnostic detection format, so it converts to Splunk, Elastic, Sentinel, or any SIEM backend. Each rule carries its ATT&CK mapping, a confidence level, and explicit false-positive notes. The coverage view turns the catalog into an honest picture of where the blue team can and cannot see.
